How PaceFetch predictions work

The goal is not to manufacture picks. The goal is to identify the strongest available opportunities.

01 / SCAN

Check availability

The system first looks at available upcoming fixtures and markets instead of forcing a fixed number of predictions.

02 / FILTER

Remove weak candidates

Matches with insufficient or conflicting information can be excluded from the public prediction feed.

03 / RANK

Calculate probability

Available form, strength, market information and other signals contribute to the model's estimated probability.

04 / PUBLISH

Show the strongest

The highest-quality qualifying predictions are published, up to a maximum of 50 per day.

Frequently asked

PaceFetch FAQ

Can PaceFetch publish 50 predictions every day?
It can publish up to 50 when 50 suitable predictions are available. The system should never manufacture weak selections simply to reach a target number.
What does an 80% confidence prediction mean?
It means the model estimates approximately an 80% probability for the selected outcome under its current inputs. It is not a guarantee.
Why are confidence levels displayed with colours?
Colour makes the probability tier easier to understand quickly: strong green for 80% and above, yellow for the 70s, amber for 50–69%, and red below 50%.
Does PaceFetch only use low odds?
No. Odds can be one input among several. The model should consider the available information and only publish a selection when its overall evidence is strong enough.
Can I use PaceFetch predictions as a guarantee?
No. Predictions are probabilistic information. Even a very high-confidence outcome can fail because football matches contain uncertainty.
