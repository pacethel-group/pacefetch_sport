PaceFetch Prediction System

Our Methodology

PaceFetch combines football data, statistical analysis and market-specific indicators to produce probability-based predictions.

How It Works

01

Match Data

We analyse available information about upcoming matches, including recent performances, home and away records, goals, results and competition context.

02

Team Performance

Recent team form is evaluated to identify patterns that may influence the probability of different outcomes.

03

Market Analysis

Different prediction markets are considered independently. A team being favoured to win does not automatically mean every other market has the same probability.

04

Probability

The available indicators are converted into probability estimates. Predictions are then ranked according to their calculated confidence.

Confidence Levels

80%+
Strong Confidence

High statistical confidence based on the available indicators.

70–79%
Good Confidence

A relatively strong probability, but still subject to match uncertainty.

50–69%
Moderate Confidence

The prediction has some statistical support but carries greater uncertainty.

<50%
Low Confidence

A weaker probability that should not be treated as a strong prediction.

Important Principles

Probability Is Not Certainty

A 90% probability does not mean an outcome is guaranteed. Football contains unpredictable events such as injuries, red cards, tactical changes and individual mistakes.

Availability Matters

PaceFetch does not need to force a fixed number of predictions when suitable matches are unavailable. The system can publish fewer predictions when qualifying fixtures are limited.

Markets Are Different

Match result, both teams to score, totals and other markets have different statistical characteristics and are evaluated separately.

Historical Results

Previous predictions can be recorded and compared with actual match results to measure the performance of the prediction system over time.

Important: PaceFetch predictions are statistical estimates for informational and entertainment purposes. They are not guarantees of match results and should never be treated as certain financial outcomes.
