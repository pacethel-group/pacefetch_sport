How PaceFetch Works

Transparent probability-based football predictions, not promises.

Probability first

PaceFetch displays model-generated probabilities from available football data. A 70% probability is not a guarantee; it is an estimate of likelihood.

Daily publishing

The prediction endpoint is requested by date. The frontend displays the current GMT date and does not manufacture picks when qualifying data is unavailable.

Confidence tiers

  • 80%+ — Strong
  • 70–79.9% — Good
  • 50–69.9% — Moderate
  • Below 50% — not displayed on the main prediction board

Performance record

Production history should be settled from actual fixture scores on the server, not from browser localStorage. This prevents visitors from changing or losing the official record.
